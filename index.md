# AgenticLMS Roadmap

AgenticLMS is a learning platform for organizations that want to create, deliver, and manage courses. This roadmap describes planned capabilities, not release commitments.

## Now — Verify the app and finalize designs

- Verify the current app and its main administrator and learner workflows.
- Finalize the design handoff for the React administrator and learner interfaces.

## Next — Pilot the new learning experience

- Prove one end-to-end workflow: an administrator publishes a course, a learner completes it, and the platform records the result.
- Connect the React interfaces to the backend. Expand administrator and learner workflows after the pilot is verified.
- Support organization-specific branding and UI languages without separate codebases. Verify that each organization can manage its own users, courses, and settings without accessing another organization's private data.
- Establish course versioning, draft-to-publish workflows, configurable pass criteria, and audit trails. Expand audit coverage beyond the pilot as features roll out.

## Later — Expand course delivery and authoring

- Deliver audio and video courses with selectable language and accent tracks. Let learners switch tracks without restarting the video, and let authors manage tracks without duplicating course content.
- Import structured Word and Excel files as course drafts that authors can review, correct, and validate before publishing.

## Later — Add integrations

- Add email, SMS, and WhatsApp notifications, beginning with one event and one channel.
- Evaluate Discourse for learner communities and add single sign-on. Bring single sign-on forward if the pilot requires it.
- Integrate with Inji Certify to issue verifiable credentials for course completion, assessments, and certifications.

Milestones may change based on pilot feedback and integration requirements. This page will be updated as priorities and progress are confirmed.
